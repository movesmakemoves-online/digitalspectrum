How it works

  1. Tell us what's eating your time. “I spend hours answering the same questions on Instagram” is enough to start with.
  2. We look at how you actually work day to day and come back with a written plan — what we'd automate, what it costs, and the time it should save you.
  3. Happy to go ahead? We build it and connect it to the tools you already use.
  4. We test it on real questions and real scenarios before it ever talks to a customer.
  5. We switch it on and show you (and your team) exactly how it works.
  6. It's yours to keep running. We can maintain and improve it, or hand over the keys.
For example: say you run a hair salon and want to stop answering the same booking questions every evening. We'd set up a chatbot that answers opening hours, prices and availability, and books appointments straight into your diary — tested on real questions before it ever talks to a customer, live within a couple of weeks.

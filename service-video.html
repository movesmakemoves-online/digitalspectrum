How it works

  1. Tell us what you've got and what it's for. “I've got footage from a gig and want a highlights reel for Instagram” is plenty.
  2. We come back with a written quote covering the edit, the style and the turnaround time.
  3. Happy to go ahead? Send over your footage (or we can film it) and we get cutting.
  4. We share a draft for you to react to before anything's locked in.
  5. We polish it — colour, sound, titles — and deliver it in whatever formats you need.
  6. It's yours to post, broadcast or send wherever you like. Need more cut down the line? We're here.
For example: say you're releasing a new single and need a music video. We'd talk through the look and feel you want, work from your footage (or help you film it), send a first cut for feedback, then finish it with colour grading and titles — ready to release in around 2 to 3 weeks.

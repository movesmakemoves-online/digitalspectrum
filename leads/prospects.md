# DSL prospects

Researched prospects for Digital Spectrum Labs' five services. Every entry records entity
type and address type, because UK PECR only allows cold email to corporate subscribers
(Ltd, LLP, CIC, public body) on a business address. Sole traders, unincorporated
partnerships and any freemail address need prior consent — for those, use their own
website form, LinkedIn, post, or phone after a TPS **and** CTPS screen.

**Nothing here has been contacted. Every message is drafted and gated on the owner's approval.**

Service landing pages (live at `digitalspectrumlabs.co.uk` — confirmed resolving 2026-09-01,
was still on `digitalspectrum.netlify.app` only as of 2026-08-14):
websites `service-websites.html` · AI `service-ai.html` · growth `service-growth.html` ·
video `service-video.html` · design `service-design.html`

## 2026-08-14

### 1. Alliance of Independent Promoters (UK) CIC — trading as Association of Independent Promoters (AIP)
- **What they do:** not-for-profit membership body for independent UK music promoters; partnered with Liveline, a national grassroots live music fund.
- **Why they fit:** their own site is a single "COMING SOON" placeholder that says *a new site is on the way with better resources, a stronger network and access to support*. They are openly mid-rebuild and have a membership base to serve — a members area, resource library and application flow is squarely DSL's web work.
- **Service:** Websites/Apps → `service-websites.html`
- **Entity type:** CIC, private company limited by guarantee — **corporate subscriber**. Companies House 14880464, incorporated 19 May 2023, active, registered office 10 Croft Meadow, Chipperfield, Kings Langley WD4 9EB. *Caveat: the site itself publishes no company number, so the match between aiporg.com and this registration is inferred from the name and the site's own "not-for-profit CIC" description, not confirmed on their page.*
- **Address type:** none published — the site has only a "Register Interest" form.
- **Lawful route:** their own Register Interest form, or LinkedIn. Cold email is legally available (corporate subscriber) but there is no address to use. Do not guess one.
- **Source:** https://www.aiporg.com/ · https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/14880464
- **Found:** 2026-08-14

### 2. DeepMatter Records Limited
- **What they do:** London independent record label, founded 2017, jazz/soul/hip-hop, 20+ artist roster, vinyl store, sister imprint Root Records and a publishing arm.
- **Why they fit:** roster of 20+ artists and an active release schedule, but the site carries **no video content at all** — static artwork only, on a stock Squarespace build. Music video and film editing is DSL's own background (20+ years media, artist development then filmmaking), so this is the strongest credibility match on the list.
- **Service:** Video Production → `service-video.html` (secondary: Growth & Marketing → `service-growth.html`)
- **Entity type:** Private limited company — **corporate subscriber**. Companies House 12261270, incorporated 14 October 2019, active, registered office 1 Frome Street, London N1 8PB.
- **Address type:** none published — contact is via a form on their site.
- **Lawful route:** contact form on deepmatter.co.uk. Email would be lawful if a business address surfaces (e.g. on a release or press sheet), but none is published — use the form.
- **Source:** https://www.deepmatter.co.uk/ · https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/12261270
- **Found:** 2026-08-14

### 3. Ground Rules Festival
- **What they do:** new grassroots UK festival for sound system culture and underground dance music. 2026 edition ran 19–21 June at Deene Park, Corby; four stages.
- **Why they fit:** the site now says *"Thank you so much to all of you who joined us for 2026… We are 100% back in 2027"* — they are in the planning window where aftermovie edits, 2027 brand assets and a ticket-driving site actually get bought. Site is a basic Wix build with tickets pushed out to Resident Advisor.
- **Service:** Video Production → `service-video.html` (secondary: Graphic Design → `service-design.html`)
- **Entity type:** **Unknown — treat as unknown.** GROUND RULES LTD (16714259, private company limited by guarantee, incorporated 12 September 2025, registered office 256 Albion Road, London N16 9JP) matches the name and timing, but the festival site publishes no company number, so the two are **not confirmed** to be the same entity.
- **Address type:** business domain — `info@groundrules.co.uk`.
- **Lawful route:** **Not email yet.** Entity unconfirmed, so corporate-subscriber status cannot be assumed. Use the mailing-list/contact route on their own site or an Instagram DM (@groundrulesfestival). Cold email to `info@` only becomes lawful once the Ltd link is confirmed — e.g. a company number on their terms, tickets or accounts page.
- **Source:** https://www.groundrules.co.uk/ · https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/16714259
- **Found:** 2026-08-14

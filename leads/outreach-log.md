# DSL Outreach Log — the three numbers, every week

Restarted 2026-09-01, per the rule in the "Six Months, Resumed" plan: **every week ends with
three numbers — approaches made, conversations had, pounds invoiced.** This is the rule that
slipped when outreach stalled, and there was no record of whether the drop-off was a bad week
or a real pattern — because the numbers weren't being kept. This file is that record, going
forward.

Log a new row every Friday (matches the existing "delivery, invoice anything finished, 30-minute
review" slot in the plan's weekly rhythm) — even when two of the three numbers are zero.

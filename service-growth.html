How it works

  1. Tell us what “more customers” looks like for you. “I want more people finding my café on Instagram” is a great starting point.
  2. We look at what you're doing already and come back with a written plan — channels, content, and a realistic idea of what it costs and what it should bring in.
  3. Happy to go ahead? We set everything up — accounts, campaigns, automations — properly, not just switched on and hoped for.
  4. We run it, post it, send it — whatever the plan calls for — and check in with you regularly.
  5. We show you what's working with plain-English reporting, not just numbers on a screen.
  6. Keep it running as a monthly retainer, or take stock of what we've built and carry it on yourself.
For example: say you run a local café and want more people finding you online. We'd start with a quick audit of your Instagram and Google listing, put together a simple content and posting plan, then start posting and tracking what actually brings people through the door — with a monthly check-in showing what's working.

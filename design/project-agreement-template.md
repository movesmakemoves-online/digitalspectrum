# Project Agreement / Statement of Work — Template

Fill in the bracketed sections per client, then send as a PDF or plain document for the
client to countersign (email confirmation is enough for smaller jobs; a signature is worth
getting for anything over ~£1,000 or a retainer). This works alongside `terms.html` on the
live site — `terms.html` covers the general rules for everyone, this document nails down the
specifics of one project.

---

## Digital Spectrum Labs — Project Agreement

**Client:** [Client legal name / business name]
**Contact:** [Name, email]
**Date:** [Date]
**Prepared by:** [Your name], Digital Spectrum Labs

### 1. What this covers
[Plain description of the project — e.g. "A 5-page business website for [Client], including
a contact form and mobile-responsive design, as scoped in the quote dated [date]."]

### 2. Deliverables
- [Deliverable 1]
- [Deliverable 2]
- [Deliverable 3]

Anything not listed here is out of scope and will be quoted separately if requested.

### 3. Price and payment
- **Total price:** £[amount]
- **Deposit:** £[50% of total] due before work begins
- **Balance:** £[remaining] due on delivery
- Payment via Stripe (card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, bank transfer, or Link) — see
  digitalspectrumlabs.co.uk/payments.html for full details.
- Late payment on the balance may pause delivery of final files until settled.

### 4. Timeline
- **Start date:** [date]
- **Estimated delivery:** [date or range]
- Timeline depends on the client providing requested content/feedback within [X] business
  days of it being asked for. Delays on the client's side may push the delivery date back.

### 5. Revisions
[Number] rounds of revisions are included, covering changes within the original scope.
Requests outside the original scope, or additional rounds beyond what's included, will be
quoted and invoiced separately.

### 6. Intellectual property
- On payment of the **final invoice in full**, ownership of the finished deliverables listed
  in Section 2 transfers to the Client.
- Digital Spectrum Labs retains ownership of any pre-existing tools, code libraries,
  templates, or components used to build the deliverable, and may reuse general techniques,
  non-client-specific code, and know-how in future projects.
- Until the final invoice is paid in full, all work remains the property of Digital Spectrum
  Labs.
- The Client warrants that any content they supply (text, images, logos, etc.) is either
  their own or properly licensed, and indemnifies Digital Spectrum Labs against any
  third-party IP claim arising from client-supplied content.

### 7. Confidentiality
Both parties agree to keep confidential any non-public business information shared during
the project, and not to disclose it to third parties without consent.

### 8. Liability
Digital Spectrum Labs' total liability under this agreement is limited to the total fees
paid for this project. Digital Spectrum Labs is not liable for indirect or consequential
loss (e.g. lost profits, lost data not caused by our negligence).

### 9. Termination
Either party may end this agreement in writing. If the Client cancels after work has begun,
the deposit is non-refundable and any work completed to date will be invoiced pro-rata.

### 10. Governing law
This agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
